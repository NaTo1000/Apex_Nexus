The **9/36design DACp Protocol** is understood to be a low-level specification for **Data-centric Asynchronous Compute and Pacing** that specifically addresses the fusion of these streams, likely by:

1.  **Optimizing NPU-GPU Data Flow:** Creating a highly efficient, low-latency channel between the Neural Processing Unit (NPU) for AI inference (e.g., object recognition, audio source separation) and the Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) for rendering and spatial audio processing.
2.  **Synchronized Pacing:** Implementing a unified timing and scheduling mechanism to ensure that visual frames and spatial audio cues are rendered and delivered with sub-millisecond synchronization, which is critical for maintaining AR immersion and preventing motion sickness.
